Aim: Compute node level measures: Centrality measures, Notion of Importance, Graph Centers, Reciprocity, Clustering.

1. Creating and displaying the graph.
CODE:
import networkx as nx
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

G = nx.Graph()
edges = [('A' , 'B'), ('A' , 'C'), ('B' , 'C'), ('B' , 'D'), ('C' , 'D'), ('D' , 'E')]
G.add_edges_from(edges)

plt.figure(figsize = (6,4))
nx.draw(G, with_labels = True, node_color = 'lightblue', node_size = 1500, font_size = 14)
plt.title("Graph")
plt.show()
2. Degree of centrality.
CODE:
#degree of centrality (v) = Degree(v)/n-1 where v = no. of connection and n = no. of nodes
deg_centrality = nx.degree_centrality(G)
print("Degree of Centrality:")
for node, cent in deg_centrality.items():
    print(f"Node {node} : {cent:.2f}")
3. Betweenness Centrality.
CODE:
bet_centrality = nx.betweenness_centrality(G)
print("Betweenness Centrality:")
for node,cent in bet_centrality.items():
    print(f"Node {node} : {cent:.2f}")
4. Closeness Centrality.
CODE:
#CLoseness Centrality (node) = n-1/n
close_centrality = nx.closeness_centrality(G)
print("Closeness Centrality:")
for node, cent in close_centrality.items():
    print(f"Node {node} : {cent:.2f}")
5. Eccentricity.
CODE:
ecc = nx.eccentricity(G)
min_ecc = min(ecc.values())
centers = [node for node, e in ecc.items() if e == min_ecc]
print("Eccentricity of Nodes:")
for node, e in ecc.items():
    print(f"Node {node} : {e}")
print(f"\nGraph Centers (min ecc = {min_ecc} : {centers})")
6. Reciprocity (Directed Graph).
CODE:
DG = G.to_directed()
recip = nx.reciprocity(DG)
print(f"Reciprocity of the directed graph : {recip:.2f}")
7. Clustering Coefficient.
CODE:
clustering = nx.clustering(G)
print("Clustering Coefficient:")
for node, coeff in clustering.items():
    print(f"Node {node} : {coeff:.2f}")
